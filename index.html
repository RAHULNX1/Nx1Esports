REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Thank you for choosing NX1 Esports. We strive to provide a fair, transparent, and competitive gaming environment. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully before registering for any tournament or match:

1. Match Cancellations & Technical Issues (Refund Eligible)

Full refunds will be credited back directly to the user's platform wallet within 24-48 hours under the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Hacker/Cheater Inbound: If a match is disrupted, ruined, or cancelled due to the presence of a hacker or cheater, and verified by our moderation team, the entry fee will be fully refunded to all affected fair players.
  • Technical Glitches: If a match cannot be completed or started due to technical glitches, server crashes, room hosting errors, or unforeseen administrative issues from NX1 Esports' end.
  • Cancellation by Platform: If a tournament is cancelled by the organizers for any other internal reason.

2. User-End Cancellations & Non-Participation (Strictly Non-Refundable)

No refunds or entry fee reversals will be provided under these conditions:

  • Self-Withdrawal: Once a player successfully pays the entry fee and reserves a slot, they cannot claim a refund if they change their mind or wish to leave the match. You must participate in the registered match.
  • No-Show / Absent: If a user registers for a match but fails to join the custom room, or misses the match start time due to personal reasons, internet issues, or device failure, their entry fee will be forfeited.
  • Disqualification: If a player is caught hacking, cheating, teaming up, or breaking the tournament rules, they will be disqualified immediately, and no refund will be issued.

3. Refund Mechanism

  • All eligible refunds (due to cancellation, hackers, or glitches) will be processed automatically and credited directly into the User's NX1 Esports Wallet.
  • Wallet balance can be used to join future tournaments listed on the platform. For any balance or transaction-related disputes, players can contact our support team through the Contact Us page with valid screenshots/proofs.
