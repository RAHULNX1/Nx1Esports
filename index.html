REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

At NX1 Esports, we ensure a fair and transparent gaming ecosystem. Please read our rules regarding refunds carefully:

  • Hacker/Cheater Policy: If any player is found using third-party hacks, ghost clients, or modifications inside the match, they will be instantly disqualified. No refunds will be provided for entry fees in this case.
  • Technical Glitches: If a tournament match is cancelled from our end due to server crashes or platform technical issues, the full registration amount will be credited back to the player's wallet instantly.
  • Player No-Show: If a player fails to join the room before the start time using their correct registered UID and Username, no refunds will be issued.
